Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Workload Allocation feature work?
Sprintly AI analyzes each team member's role (Developer, QA Engineer, Designer, Architect), weekly capacity, and current active tickets. It matches ticket story points and type to balance the load evenly across all engineers with 1-click commitment.
Does the Gantt Chart update in real-time?
Yes! The Gantt Chart connects to our high-velocity REST API stream and auto-syncs every few seconds. Any status transition on your Kanban board immediately reflects on your roadmap timeline.
How is user authentication secured?
Sprintly uses 6-digit email OTP (One-Time Password) verification for both signups and logins. Passwords are hash-protected with PBKDF2, sessions are encrypted, and API routes are guarded with sliding-window rate limiters.
Can I switch between Light and Dark mode?
Yes, you can toggle between Light and Dark mode using the sun/moon button in the top navigation bar at any time. Your preference is persisted across all pages.
